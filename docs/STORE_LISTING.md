# DreamShelf - Store Listing Information

## App Name
DreamShelf - Book Tracker & Reading Journal

## Short Description (80 characters max)
Track your reading progress, set goals, and build your dream book collection.

## Full Description

📚 **DreamShelf** is your ultimate book tracking companion, designed for passionate readers who want to organize their reading life and achieve their literary goals.

### 📖 TRACK YOUR READING
- Add books by scanning ISBN barcodes or searching online
- Track reading progress with page counts
- Log reading sessions and monitor time spent
- See your reading speed and statistics

### 🎯 SET & ACHIEVE GOALS
- Create daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly reading goals
- Track pages read and books completed
- Build reading streaks and maintain consistency
- Earn achievement badges for milestones

### 📊 POWERFUL STATISTICS
- View reading trends with beautiful charts
- See books by status, genre, and category
- Track your reading streak
- Monitor pages and books read over time

### 🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS & BADGES
- Unlock badges as you reach reading milestones
- Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers
- Celebrate your reading accomplishments

### 📱 BEAUTIFUL INTERFACE
- Material 3 design with beautiful animations
- 3D bookshelf view for your library
- Hero animations for smooth transitions
- Dark mode support

### ✨ PREMIUM FEATURES
- Unlimited books (Free: 20 books)
- Custom collections and tags
- Premium color themes
- Ad-free experience
- Export your library data

### 🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
- All data stored locally on your device
- No account required
- Your reading habits stay private

Whether you're a casual reader or a bookworm, DreamShelf helps you:
✓ Stay organized with your TBR (To Be Read) list
✓ Remember what you've read
✓ Discover patterns in your reading habits
✓ Stay motivated with goals and achievements

Download DreamShelf today and start your reading journey!
